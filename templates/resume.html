About Me

Hi! I'm Arsen Smetaniak, a software engineering student, indie game developer, and creator who loves crafting engaging gameplay and robust applications.

I am currently pursuing my Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering at Vasyl Stefanyk Carpathian National University (2024–2028), having previously graduated from Lyceum "Intellect" (2020–2024). My academic foundation combines solid theoretical principles with real-world engineering practices.

As an indie developer, I specialize in the Godot Engine (GDScript, GDShader) and backend development with Java, Spring, and SQL. I have authored and published games including Our Squishy Hero and Our Perfect Spot, along with developer tools such as the AreaOfSight2D plugin. I take pride in my strong problem-solving capabilities, creativity, adaptability, and dedication to effective teamwork.
